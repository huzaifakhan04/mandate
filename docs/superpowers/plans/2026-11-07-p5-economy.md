# P5 Economy Harness Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development.

**Goal:** Make the bank a living economy:
- a seeded base rate and inflation walk;
- utilization-based dynamic pricing with a grade premium;
- a CECL-style allowance;
- a treasury policy (reserve floor, concentration limit, lending budget, redemption gate) with a daily AI treasury memo;
- stress scenarios (default wave, note run, rate spike) that judges can trigger in private worlds;
- a richer bot economy.

**Architecture:**
- Each world has a daily `economy_state` row. It is computed by `stepEconomy(db, worldId, simDay)` at each sim-day boundary: the tick in the main world, and advance in private worlds. Runs are seeded and deterministic per world.
- Pricing v2's base rate (P3: a constant of 400 bps) comes from the latest `economy_state`.
- Treasury policy checks run inside the existing locks (series lock) as part of underwriting and redemption.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-mandate-design.md` (P5 row; "utilization-based dynamic rates + per-grade premium; simulated base rate + inflation with real returns; treasury/ROI policy with CECL-style reserve and concentration limits; stress scenarios"). Rulings: P1–P4 ledgers and `docs/superpowers/plans/p1-rulings.md`.

## Global Constraints
- **Earlier rules apply:**
  - integer cents and bps;
  - no PayPal or OpenAI call inside a transaction;
  - lock order series → loan → positions → agent;
  - main world on PayPal, private worlds on sim;
  - decisions deterministic, with the LLM writing text only;
  - commit trailer is `Co-Authored-By` only.
- **Base rate walk:**
  - Start at 400 bps.
  - Each sim day, add a seeded step in −25..+25 bps, plus mean reversion of −10% × (rate − 400), clamped to 100..1200.
  - Inflation index starts at 10000 (= 1.0000) and grows by 2–6% a year, applied daily via the seeded walk.
  - The real return to holders (nominal minus inflation) is shown in `get_statement` and the console.
- **Utilization** = loans_receivable outstanding ÷ (cash_paypal + loans_receivable), in bps.
  - Utilization premium: 0 bps below 50%; then linear to +600 bps at 90%; above 90%, new loans are declined with `pool_utilization_high`.
- **Pricing v2 cost of funds:** base rate + utilization premium, replacing P3's fixed 400. The grade premium already exists through PD/LGD. Disclosure v2 adds `base_rate_bps` and `utilization_premium_bps` (hashed). Offers keep their quoted terms.
- **CECL allowance:** each day, target allowance = Σ over open loans (outstanding principal × PD(grade at offer) × LGD) plus a 2× multiplier for late and substandard loans.
  - Book the delta: Dr `provision_expense` / Cr `allowance_for_losses` (or the reverse).
  - Charge-off (P4) uses the allowance first. Reconcile with P4's direct provision journal: P4 provisions the full principal at charge-off. P5 changes this to release the existing allowance for that loan and provision only the shortfall. The total expense is the same and the trial balance stays 0.
  - The holders' loss share is unchanged.
- **Treasury policy** (per world, stored in `economy_state` or a `treasury_policy` table with defaults):
  - **Reserve floor:** cash ≥ 15% of notes_payable. Loans that would breach it are declined with `reserve_floor`, and redemptions are queued with a `redemption_gated` status.
  - **Concentration:** one principal's exposure ≤ 30% of total loans plus available liquidity. Breaching it declines with `concentration_limit`.
  - **Daily lending budget:** max new principal per sim day = 40% of available liquidity at day start. Exceeding it declines with `daily_budget_exhausted`.
  - **Redemption gate:** when cash falls below the floor, new redemptions get status `queued` (FIFO). The tick processes them when cash recovers. The holder is told why and when.
- **AI treasury memo:** a daily `treasury_officer` role, using the P3 AI layer. Its input is the economy numbers only. It outputs `{headline, observations[], risks[]}`, advisory and shown in the console. It runs after commit in the tick and is capped at 1 per world per sim day. Bots' private worlds use templates, to save cost.
- **Stress scenarios** (private worlds only; an owner button on `/dashboard/worlds/[id]` plus an internal API):
  - `default_wave`: the next N borrower-bot loans skip repayment.
  - `note_run`: all lender bots request full redemption.
  - `rate_spike`: the base rate jumps +500 bps, then mean-reverts.
  - Each scenario is an `events` row with seeded parameters; the outcome is visible in the console (P6).
- **Bots v2:** more bots per world (4 lenders, 6 borrowers). Borrower behaviours are parameterised: good payer, late payer (+3 days), defaulter (10% chance). Lenders respond to rate and yield: they buy more when the real return is above 2% and redeem when it is below 0.
- **Invariants checked after every simulated day in tests:**
  - trial balance 0;
  - Ruling T (`notes_payable` = units);
  - allowance ≥ 0;
  - cash ≥ 0;
  - utilization ≤ 100%;
  - no PayPal-rail operations in private worlds.

## Review Focus
1. **Determinism:** the same seed and the same actions produce an identical economy history and ledger.
2. **Treasury gates under concurrency:** budget, floor and concentration are checked under the series lock, so two accepts can't jointly breach them.
3. **CECL plus P4 charge-off interaction:** no double provisioning; total provision expense equals the loss after the allowance release.
4. **Queued redemptions:** they never pay more than cash; FIFO order is kept; a holder's loss-sharing (P4-D deferred) still applies to queued redemptions.
5. **Stress scenarios:** they cannot be triggered in the main world or for another owner's world.
